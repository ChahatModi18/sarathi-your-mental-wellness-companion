1. INTRODUCTION
1. INTRODUCTION
1.1. Problem Statement
Many individuals face significant challenges in identifying, tracking, and effectively
reflecting on their emotional states during daily life. Current digital journaling tools often
lack the necessary personalization and cultural relevance, leading to low user engagement
and limited emotional growth. Furthermore, existing applications frequently fail to detect
critical crisis moments or provide specialized support for specific demographics, such as
women’s health tracking. A research gap identified through the study of over five
academic papers highlights that most sentiment analysis models are trained on social
media data rather than private entries, reducing their accuracy in detecting genuine
depressive patterns.
1.2 Overview
The BhagvadMood Journal (also known as SarathiApp) is a comprehensive, full-stack,
emotion-aware spiritual companion designed to bridge the gap between modern wellness
technology and ancient Vedic wisdom. Developed as an offline desktop application, it
provides a secure and private environment for users to document their thoughts without
the privacy concerns associated with cloud-based platforms. The system functions as a
holistic wellness tool that integrates mental health tracking with physical health
monitoring, specifically addressing the intersection of hormonal cycles and emotional
well-being.
1.3 Objectives
The primary goals of this project are:

To build a robust Tkinter-based desktop application for daily emotional
journaling.

To utilize Natural Language Processing (NLP) through TextBlob to accurately
classify user moods from raw text inputs.


To provide spiritually resonant guidance by mapping detected moods to specific
Bhagavad Gita shlokas and positive cognitive reframes.

To implement specialized tracking for the female menstrual cycle, including
symptoms, flow, and energy levels, to offer holistic health insights.

To enhance user engagement through gamified features like the "Spin Wheel" for
motivational boosts and a structured To-Do list for task clarity.

To ensure data privacy through a localized MySQL database, allowing for offline
statistics and entry management.
1.4 Solution
The BhagvadMood Journal provides a personalized, offline solution that uses advanced
sentiment analysis to contextualize a user's emotional state. Unlike generic tools, it offers
a "Safety-First" approach with hardcoded, priority regex-based crisis detection to
immediately redirect users in distress to helplines. By automating the "emotion
reframing" process through the lens of the Bhagavad Gita, the application offers a unique,
culturally tailored support system that fosters resilience and self-reflection. The
integration of physical health logs and visual mood analytics ensures a complete holistic
view of the user’s well-being.

6. CONCLUSION
The BhagvadMood Journal (SarathiApp) serves as a much-needed digital solution to the
longstanding problem of generic and culturally detached mental health tools. By providing a
transparent, offline platform, the system ensures high data privacy while reducing the risk of
crisis moments going undetected through priority regex-based safety protocols.
This minor project demonstrates the practical application of Computer Science and
Engineering principles—specifically Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Database
Management—in solving real-world wellness issues. It fills a critical research gap by
moving beyond simple rule-based systems to provide culturally resonant insights that
contextualize emotional states via Bhagavad Gita wisdom.
The system has strong potential for future enhancements, including:

LLM-Powered Conversational Support: Integrating agents like Gemini for
dynamic, empathetic user interactions.

Cloud Synchronization: Enabling cross-platform integration and secure clinician
access.

Advanced Mood Prediction: Utilizing deep learning models for context-aware
emotional detection.

Wearable Integration: Capturing physical stress data from smart devices for a more
holistic health analysis.
By blending ancient tradition with modern technology, this project provides a scalable and
interactive foundation for holistic personal growth and emotional resilie
